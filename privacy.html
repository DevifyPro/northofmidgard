North of Midgard Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

North of Midgard Studio is a private tool that the creator of the North of Midgard history channel uses to send their own videos to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data the tool handles when it connects to TikTok.

What the tool collects

When the account owner connects a TikTok account, TikTok shares:

  • the account's basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar), so the tool can show which account is connected;
  • an access token and refresh token, so the tool can post videos to that account, or send them to its TikTok inbox.

The tool does not collect followers, messages, contacts, location, viewing activity or any data about other TikTok users.

How the data is used

The data is used only to post videos made by the account owner to their own TikTok account. Each post is started by the owner on the tool's posting screen, where they choose the caption, who can view it and the other post settings; or the video is sent to the owner's TikTok inbox to finish in the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and it is never sold.

Where the data is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored only on the account owner's own computer, in the tool's local settings. They are not sent to any server other than TikTok's own API.

Sharing

Data is shared with nobody except TikTok, as needed to perform the upload.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept until the account is disconnected. To remove it, disconnect the account in the tool, or revoke the tool's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless immediately.

Children

The tool is not meant for, and does not knowingly handle data from, anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated.

Contact
